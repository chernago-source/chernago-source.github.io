About the Gallery

Healing Moments is a nature photo gallery created by Skylar Chernago. This project is dedicated to her mother, Kara Stone, a beloved mother whose love and strength continue to grow and prosper like nature.

Why Nature?

Nature can bring comfort in difficult times. The images in this collection are meant to help viewers slow down, breathe, and find a small moment of peace.

Intended purpose

  • Create a calm, enjoyable form of scrolling without socil media or advertisements
  • Practice responsive web design (mobile and desktop)
  • Provide reliable resources for learning and supportrelated to Cancer.
